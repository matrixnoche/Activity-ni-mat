Hero of the Shadows

A prodigy of Konohagakure's Uchiha clan, Itachi served as an Anbu Captain before accepting the ultimate burden: sacrificing his honor, family, and reputation to protect his younger brother Sasuke and prevent a global shinobi war.

The 6 Chapters of Itachi

Explore the definitive milestones in the life of the Sharingan master

1. Prodigy of the Leaf

From mastering the Sharingan at an early age to graduating at the top of his academy class, young Itachi views the world with unprecedented philosophical depth.

2. Anbu Operations

Rising through the ranks to lead squad operations in secret, Itachi balances loyalty between the Uchiha Clan hierarchy and the Hidden Leaf high council.

3. The Fateful Night

Facing an impossible tragedy, Itachi makes the ultimate sacrifice to prevent an all-out civil war, leaving behind a single vow to his younger brother.

4. Shadows of Akatsuki

Operating under the rogue cloak alongside Kisame Hoshigaki, Itachi keeps watch over the ninja world from within the dark organization.

5. Brotherly Destiny

The long-awaited confrontation unfolds. Bound by fate and illusion, Itachi pushes Sasuke to his limits to unleash his true strength.

6. Reanimation & Truth

Returned to the living world under Edo Tenseial, Itachi breaks free to alter the course of the Great Shinobi War and reveal his unconditional love.
